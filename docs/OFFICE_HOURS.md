# Office Hours diagnostic

## Position
Build only as a one-city paid editorial tool. A nationwide civic search platform is a trap: incumbents own the corpus, search demand is zero, and the budgeted residents'-association segment is thinner than the pitch suggests.

1. **Demand reality:** People spend hours attending and summarizing meetings, and residents complain about multi-year delays. That proves pain. No cited association has offered to pay, so demand remains unvalidated.
2. **Painful status quo:** A local civic writer watches VODs, searches agendas/minutes, keeps links in notes, and manually explains a three-year sequence to readers. The cost is six meetings/week plus credibility risk if a status is wrong.
3. **Desperate customer:** The first customer is one named-type role: the solo civic Substack operator already publishing weekly in one city. Their job is trust and continuity; missing a reversal loses subscribers.
4. **Narrowest paid wedge:** Five maintained, embeddable timelines with dated primary-source links and a weekly change digest for $49/month. Do not build broad transcript search.
5. **Observation plan:** Recruit three active civic writers. Give each a cold prototype and one real issue; observe without coaching. Measure whether they find the current status, challenge a source, add a missing event, and embed/share the result. Ask for $49 before building ingestion.
6. **Future-fit:** As commodity transcription expands, raw summaries get cheaper and less differentiated. Verified continuity becomes more valuable only if named local editors own corrections and provenance.

## Approaches considered
- **Minimal viable (chosen):** Manual editor + five timelines + sourced events. Small effort, medium editorial risk, fastest payment test.
- **Hybrid ingestion:** Automated agenda/VOD parsing feeding a human review queue. Medium effort/high source-variance risk; add only after payment.
- **National civic graph:** Shared entity/event graph across cities. XL effort/high risk; ideal data architecture later, wrong validation vehicle now.

## Assignment
Secure one $49/month operator commitment from an existing local civic publisher using a real six-event timeline before adding automated ingestion or city two.
